# Sample product FAQ

## Warranty

The standard warranty covers manufacturing defects for two years from the purchase date. It does not cover accidental damage, normal wear, or repairs made by an unauthorized service provider. Customers can start a warranty claim by contacting support with their order number and a description of the issue.

## Returns

Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The customer should include the order number when contacting support. Custom-made products are not eligible for return unless they arrive damaged.

## Battery care

For the longest battery life, keep the device between 20% and 80% charge during everyday use. Avoid leaving it in a hot car or charging it under bedding.
